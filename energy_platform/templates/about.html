Project Overview

What is EnergyAI?

EnergyAI is an intelligent energy management platform that leverages the power of Groq's ultra-fast LLM inference to provide real-time energy consumption predictions, anomaly detection, and actionable optimization recommendations.

Built as part of an Agentic AI individual project, this platform demonstrates how modern AI can be applied to solve real-world energy efficiency challenges in industrial and commercial settings.
